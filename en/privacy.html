Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Citizo helps you prepare for the French civic exam. The app asks for no account and keeps your progress on your phone. We run no server that receives your answers, we use no advertising or analytics tools, and we sell no data.

The short version. Your application type, exam date, answers and results stay on your device. The app connects to the internet only for purchases, handled by Apple and RevenueCat, and when you open a link to an official source yourself.

1. Who we are

Citizo is published by Artiom Gaureac (“we”, “us”). For any question about your data, email gaureac@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Using the app creates information that is saved in its private storage, on your phone only:

  • the application you prepare for (multi-year residence permit, resident card or naturalisation), your exam date and your daily plan;
  • your answers, series, mock exams and their results, and the schedule of questions to review;
  • your preferences: interface language, appearance, vibrations, showing answers and the reminder time.

This information is not sent to us and we have no access to it. Deleting the app erases it.

3. No account, no ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising or analytics tools. We do not measure what you do in the app, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

The one exception, stated plainly: our purchase provider shows us statistics about the purchases themselves (how many subscriptions are active, started or cancelled). That is what Apple’s privacy label calls “Analytics”, and we declare it there. It concerns purchases, never your answers.

4. When the app connects to the internet

The questions ship inside the app, which works offline. It connects only in these cases:

PurposeWhat is sentTo whom
Showing prices, buying, restoring a purchase Transaction details and an anonymous identifier the app generates at random. No name or email address. Apple and RevenueCat
Opening a question’s source, if you tap the link Your browser opens the official page cited (for example formation-civique.interieur.gouv.fr). That website

5. Reminders

If you turn reminders on, they are scheduled on your phone. No notification goes through our servers: we have none. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

6. Service providers

  • Apple: payments, subscriptions and refunds. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: validates and restores your purchases with an anonymous identifier, and shows us statistics about them. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing.

7. If you email us

When you email us, for instance to report a mistake in a question, we receive your address and your message. We use them only to reply and to fix the app, and we delete the exchange on request.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, its correction or its erasure. Since your practice data stays on your device, you control it directly. For purchases, email us and we will pass your request on to RevenueCat. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

9. Children

Citizo is meant for adults preparing for the civic exam. It does not knowingly collect any data from children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a new version of the app handles data differently, this page is updated before that version is released.
